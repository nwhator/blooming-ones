## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, editorial, and institutionally credible visual narrative tailored for global donors, institutional partners, grassroot communities, and young beneficiaries. It balances human tenderness with resolute authority.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Dignified & Hopeful:** Eradicates clinical and stigmatizing tropes surrounding menstrual health and girl-child advocacy; frames every touchpoint with grace, agency, and celebration.
- **Institutional Rigor:** Delivers the governance, transparency, and data clarity required by global grantmakers and philanthropic foundations.
- **Organic & Grounded:** Rooted in terrestrial warmth, botanical flourishing, and natural African landscapes without resorting to visual clichés.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Organicism
The interface relies on an editorial layout structure infused with organic warmth:
- Rich, unhurried typography pairing a literary display serif with a welcoming, highly legible geometric sans.
- Ample canvas breathing room, layered warm paper-like surfaces, and gentle organic container contours.
- Subtle botanical structural accents (petal-soft pill tabs, warm hairline borders, and muted leafy markers).
- Zero cold greys; every tone carries warmth, earth, and vitality.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the deep majesty of rich plum and balanced with tender rose tones, leafy botanical greens, and comforting cream grounds.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#4A1228` - Imperial Plum):** The anchor for brand authority, key action buttons, bold display headlines, and high-emphasis navigational surfaces. It asserts prestige and unshakable purpose.
- **Secondary (`#D97D8A` - Blooming Rose):** Conveys humanity, warmth, and supportive care. Deployed for subtle highlights, interactive hovers, pill backgrounds, and empathetic callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#4A6B53` - Botanical Leaf):** Evokes community renewal, sustainable growth, and outreach. Used for metrics badges, impact validation, positive progress states, and organic foliage accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Alabaster):** The principal canvas background. Layered with `#F5F0E8` (Pressed Linen) for surface containers and elevated cards to provide tactile, paper-like warmth.
- **Neutral Text (`#24161E` - Plum Charcoal):** High-contrast text color retaining a soft warmth that avoids the harshness of pure black while strictly meeting WCAG AAA contrast ratios over cream backdrops.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF7F2`
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Level 2 (Inset Areas, Wells):** `#F5F0E8`
- **Border / Hairline Accent:** `#EADBCE` (soft terracotta cream)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges historic gravitas and compassionate accessibility. 

- **Playfair Display** serves as the voice of authority, vision, and advocacy. Used for editorial hero statements, key impact quotes, and primary section headers. It demands respectful pause and reflects human dignity.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers warm, hyper-legible utility for narrative reports, field dispatches, data visualizations, and interactive components. Its open counters ensure comfortable reading even in dense grant proposals or mobile field conditions.

### Editorial Treatments
- `label-caps` must always be styled with `letter-spacing: 0.08em` and `text-transform: uppercase` to serve as dignified eyebrow headers above major headlines.
- `display-hero` can incorporate italicized emphasis on evocative words (e.g., *bloom*, *dignity*, *future*) to cultivate an editorial, human touch.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in open, deliberate spacing that communicates credibility, organizational calm, and unhurried editorial pacing.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive layout with max content constraint at `1280px`. Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer canvas margins set to `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 639px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Section spacing favors substantial vertical boundaries (`space-xl` scaled up to `5rem` / `80px` on desktop) to allow stories of girls and community impact to be received with focus and reverence.
- Container padding balances comfortable breathing room (`space-lg`) without inflating data cards unnecessarily.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid synthetic, glossy tech aesthetics, depth is achieved through **tonal paper layering** and **ambient plum-tinted warmth**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Canvas Tier (Lowest):** `#FAF7F2` - The primary warm cream ground.
2. **Subtle Recessed Tier:** `#F5F0E8` - Used for quiet groupings, donor quote callouts, and tabular data containers. Framed by a soft 1px hairline border of `#EADBCE`.
3. **Card & Surface Tier:** `#FFFFFF` - White resting on cream creates a natural, crisp layer of elevation without artificial drop-shadows. Accompanied by a 1px border in `#EEDBDE` (subtle rose-cream).
4. **Floating Interactive Tier (Modals, Dropdowns, Raised Cards):** Elevated via soft, ultra-diffused plum ambient shadows:
   - `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 18, 40, 0.07), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 18, 40, 0.04)`
   - Never use harsh grey or black shadows; all shadows carry the warmth of the plum primary.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs **soft, welcoming geometry with organic curves**, reflecting the natural contours of petals, seeds, and open hands.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Standard Cards & Modules:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) creates gentle, approachable containment without feeling juvenile.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Full pill-shaped styling (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provides a friendly, welcoming physical touch.
- **Text Inputs & Form Elements:** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) maintains crisp alignment and serious institutional data entry feel.
- **Hero Imagery:** Rounded with an organic asymmetric treatment (e.g., top-left and bottom-right at `2rem`, top-right and bottom-left at `1rem`) to echo botanical shapes and human naturalness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Donate / Partner):** Deep Plum (`#4A1228`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Full pill curvature (`rounded-full`). Padding: `12px 28px`. Hover: `#5C1D38` with subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action (Learn More / Impact Reports):** Soft warm rose background (`#F4C2C2` at 30% opacity), deep plum text (`#4A1228`), border 1px solid `#D97D8A`. Full pill curvature.
- **Tertiary Action (Text / In-line):** Deep plum text with subtle leaf green (`#4A6B53`) underline on hover.

### Cards
- **Impact & Narrative Cards:** White background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#EADBCE` on a `#FAF7F2` canvas. Padding: `28px`. Incorporates generous vertical line-height for storytelling.
- **Data & Metric Cards:** Pressed Linen background (`#F5F0E8`), featuring a large statistic set in *Playfair Display* (Imperial Plum) anchored by a botanical leaf green pill label indicating validation or growth.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category Chips:** Pill shape with `8px 14px` padding.
  - *Menstrual Health:* Rose tint background (`#FAF0F0`), border `#E8A598`, text `#5C1D38`.
  - *Education & Outreach:* Leaf tint background (`#F0F5F1`), border `#608066`, text `#2A4531`.
  - *Governance & Reports:* Cream tint background (`#F5F0E8`), border `#D8CCBF`, text `#3D2B35`.

### Form Controls
- **Input Fields:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px `#D8CCBF` outline. Padding: `12px 16px`. Text in `#24161E`. On focus: border shifts to `#4A1228` with a 3px soft rose focus halo (`rgba(217, 125, 138, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved edges (`4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios). Border in `#5C1D38`. Checked state: solid `#4A1228` fill with clean white interior glyphs.

### Donor & Community Specific Components
- **Gift Matrix Selector:** Grid of tactile pill cards for donation tiers ($25, $50, $100, $250) displaying both monetary amount and tangible impact (e.g., "Provides 1 year of dignifying kits for 4 girls"). Selected state activates deep plum background with white text.
- **Community Quote Vignette:** Card framed with an organic plum quotation mark, warm serif body copy, and a subtle botanical leaf badge indicating the region (e.g., "Nairobi Outreach", "Entebbe Hub").
