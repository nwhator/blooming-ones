Evolution of Purpose

Three Years of Growing Together:
From Humble Outreaches to Lasting Change

Founded initially as The Testybam Initiative, our grassroots work started with a singular, urgent calling: no girl should ever choose between her education and basic menstrual dignity. As our sisterhood expanded across public secondary schools, underserved neighborhoods, and orphanages, we evolved into The Blooming Ones Initiative—a dedicated, multi-dimensional movement committed to nourishing the intellect, dignity, and inner strength of young African women.
