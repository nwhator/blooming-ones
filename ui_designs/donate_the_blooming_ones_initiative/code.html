Frequently Asked Questions

We value every kobo and dollar entrusted to our girls. Here is how your generosity is protected.

language

Can I donate from outside Nigeria?

Yes! Select the Flutterwave or Paystack tab to donate via any international Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or USD direct bank wire. Amounts are automatically converted at real-time interbank rates without punitive markup fees.

receipt_long

How will I know my gift reached the girls?

Monthly sustainers and donors receive quarterly visual briefs detailing exact distribution quantities, beneficiary letters, and high-resolution photo essays directly to their inbox, alongside verified financial balance sheets.

verified_user

What safeguarding protocols are in place?

All our field coordinators, nurse-mentors, and volunteer chaperones are rigorously vetted, police-background verified, and trained in strict child protection policies under international safeguarding treaties.

cancel

Can I pause or cancel a recurring donation?

Immediately and at any time. Every recurring notification includes an instant one-click management link, or you can email our support concierge who will modify your frequency without questions.
