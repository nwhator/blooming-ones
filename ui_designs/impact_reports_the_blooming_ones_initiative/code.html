Rigorous Methodology

How We Measure Without Compromising Dignity

Statistical integrity must never undermine adolescent privacy. Our four-tier evaluation framework protects student safety while providing institutional-grade verification.

01

Baseline Need Mapping

Collaborating strictly with female faculty heads to quantify washroom sanitation infrastructure, student intake rosters, and existing stigma markers prior to kit deployment.

checklist Ethical Needs Audits
02

Unobtrusive Tracking

Monitoring daily attendance registries and dispensary restock velocities with non-identifying telemetry, safeguarding every girl against invasive or clinical scrutiny.

lock Anonymized Data Roster
03

Independent Audits

Bi-annual fiscal and programmatic governance reviews by chartered accounting bodies and child safeguarding advisory committees to attest fund deployment.

verified_user Third-Party Verification
04

Beneficiary Voice Loops

Locked anonymous suggestion receptacles and open mic segments during community hangouts empower girls to critique and reshape our supplies and curriculums directly.
