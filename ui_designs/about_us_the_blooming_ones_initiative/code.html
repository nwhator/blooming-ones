From The Testybam Initiative to The Blooming Ones

In early 2023, our work began unassumingly as The Testybam Initiative. Witnessing bright, promising schoolgirls consistently missing a week of classes every month due to untreated menstrual stigma and lack of basic hygiene products across peri-urban settlements in Lagos and Ogun State, we started packing sanitary packs by hand in a living room.

However, as our teams sat on classroom benches with these girls, we listened deeper. We learned that period poverty was only the tangible layer of a wider crisis: early domestic pressure, absence of trusted mental health spaces, educational discouragement, and silence surrounding young womanhood.

Supplying sanitary kits was a life-saving start, but safeguarding their futures required an enduring ecosystem of edification, mentorship, and communal love. In late 2024, our community formally evolved into The Blooming Ones Initiative — broadening our horizons to nurture mind, body, and spirit across Nigeria and beyond.

"Every girl possesses innate dignity and brilliance. When we meet her basic physical health needs and surround her with loving guidance, she doesn't merely survive — she blooms."
— Tobiloba Bamgboye, Founder & Executive Director

Progressive Footprint

Our 3-Year Evolution

Trace how a grassroots weekend outreach blossomed into an institution impacting school systems across multiple states.

Year 01 • 2023 2023

The Spark: Testybam Initiative

Started by packing 500 hygiene kits by hand in a living room. We visited 3 public secondary schools in Lagos peri-urban settlements, introducing our very first menstrual hygiene classes and addressing period absenteeism head-on.

check_circle 500 Hygiene Care Kits packed
check_circle 3 Public Schools in Lagos State
Year 02 • 2024 2024

Deepening Roots & Girls' Hangouts

Recognized that health kits alone were not enough. We launched the signature Girls' Hangouts, bringing certified psychological counselors, academic mentors, and peer facilitators. Expanded outreaches to schools in Ogun and Abuja FCT.

check_circle 5,000+ adolescent girls edified
check_circle Launch of bi-monthly Safe Hangouts
Present & Next • 2025–2026 2026

Flourishing as The Blooming Ones

Official institutional rebranding and formal non-profit registration. Built 40+ permanent School Pad Dispensaries, emergency health safety funds, and established cross-state partnerships reaching over 12,500 young women.
