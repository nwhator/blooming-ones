Pillar 01 water_drop Physical Health & Hygiene

Menstrual Health & Dignity Initiative

Menstruation should never cost a girl her education or her pride. Through culturally sensitive Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) workshops, uninhibited puberty talks, and clean pad dispensary banks, we dismantle stigma and turn shame into celebration.

account_tree Programmatic Architecture

Our 4-Stage Theory of Change

Sustainable transformation is never transactional. We adhere to an empathetic, sequential model that ensures every young girl moves from survival to self-actualization.

01 hearing

Listen & Assess

We conduct community townhalls, mapping local taboos, school attendance leakages, and specific WASH infrastructure gaps directly with girls and teachers.

Phase: Diagnosis
02 healing

Provide Dignity

Immediate alleviation of shame: distribution of comprehensive dignity kits, sanitary pads, soap, clean undergarments, and anatomical booklets.

Phase: Relief & Hygiene
03 diversity_1

Continuous Mentorship

Bi-weekly interactive circles, career mentoring, emotional counseling, and leadership building that establish long-term relational resilience.

Phase: Capacity Building
04 spa

Community Autonomy

Alumnae girls become peer mentors; parent-teacher associations maintain local pad banks; community champions sustain the culture of protection.

Phase: Sustainability
