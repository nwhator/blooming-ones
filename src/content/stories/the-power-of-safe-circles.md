In many conservative households and overcrowded secondary schools, adolescent girls lack quiet, safe venues to ask questions about emotional transitions, bodily changes, peer pressure, or reproductive health. Fear of moral chastisement frequently drives questions into silence or misinformed gossip.

Every other Saturday, our **Girls' Hangouts** transform community centers and open courtyards into sanctuary spaces.

### What Happens in the Circle

Sitting on woven mats in an equal circle without desks or hierarchical platforms, facilitators guide open dialogues around:
- **Bodily Sovereignty & Puberty:** Demystifying hormonal cycles, pain management, and personal hygiene.
- **Mental Resilience & Agency:** Navigating academic stress, self-image in the digital era, and boundary-setting.
- **Career Pathways & Dream Mapping:** Identifying practical trajectories beyond traditional gender stereotypes.

### Creating Lifelong Sisterhood

"When a girl realizes she is not strange, broken, or alone in her fears, something shifts in her posture," says founder Temitope Babatunde. "She sits taller. She speaks her mind with clarity. That is what blooming looks like in practice."
