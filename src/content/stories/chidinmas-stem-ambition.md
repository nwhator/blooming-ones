Growing up in a low-income suburb of Lagos, Chidinma was repeatedly told that technical sciences were reserved for boys. Despite topping her class in further mathematics and physics, financial strain and self-doubt almost persuaded her to abandon her tertiary aspirations after senior secondary school.

That changed when she joined **The Blooming Ones Girls' Hangout** in 2024.

### A Safe Space to Dream and Prepare

Our Girls' Hangouts are designed as welcoming, judgment-free weekend gatherings where young women engage in holistic personal development. We combine life-skills mentorship with academic coaching, university entrance exam workshops, and exposure to female leaders across diverse professions.

Chidinma was paired with Dr. Funke Bakare, an advisory board member and seasoned systems engineer. Over nine months of weekly mentorship, Chidinma received guidance on university application essays, scholarship preparation, and interview practice.

### Crossing the Threshold

In November 2025, Chidinma received her official admission letter into the Department of Computer Engineering at the University of Lagos, supported by a merit micro-grant arranged through our partner network.

> "At the Girls' Hangout, I realized that I wasn't an anomaly for loving coding and mathematics," Chidinma shared during her matriculation ceremony. "The mentors looked me in the eye and affirmed that I belonged in the tech industry. They didn't just give advice; they held my hand until I believed in my own mind."
