Institutional childcare homes provide vital sanctuary for orphaned and abandoned children, yet many operate with strained monthly budgets. For residential caregivers, providing regular menstrual kits and personal hygiene items for dozens of maturing teenage girls is a constant financial battle.

During our December 2025 outreach, The Blooming Ones Initiative visited the Ebenezer Motherless Babies & Children's Home in Ikorodu.

### Nourishing Beyond the Basics

Our care outreach extends beyond mere material drop-offs. The volunteer medical team conducted confidential health check-ins, addressed adolescent skincare and gynecological hygiene questions, and provided each girl with personal hygiene packs containing pads, underwear, body soaps, and grooming items.

Grace, a sixteen-year-old resident with dreams of becoming a pediatric nurse, spent the afternoon alongside our volunteer doctor.

> "Many visitors bring general provisions like rice and oil, which we are deeply grateful for," explained the home matron. "However, The Blooming Ones remembered our girls' specific, intimate dignities. Having female doctors talk with them about their bodies with tenderness and respect touched everyone deeply."

Through sustained community partnerships, The Blooming Ones has added Ebenezer Home to our permanent quarterly supply network, ensuring recurring dignity deliveries throughout 2026.
